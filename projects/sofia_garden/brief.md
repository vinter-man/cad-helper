# Brief: Sofia garden

Recorded from the first conversation (the session shown in the README demo).

## Site
- Plot 12 x 18 m, rectangular, flat. North is up.
- House along the north side, 10 x 6 m, entrance in the middle of the south facade.
- Street and gate on the south side, towards the east corner.
- Sofia, Bulgaria: continental climate, hot summers, winters down to about -15 °C (USDA 6b).
- Full sun across the garden; the house shades only the strip right in front of it in winter.

## People and use
- Two adults and a dog.
- Grill with friends on summer evenings, so the terrace needs light.
- Neighbours close on the east side: want a screen there.

## Style
- Japanese, calm, not cluttered.
- Must have: a small pond, a place to grill.
- Dog: needs a bit of soft ground; gravel areas will get dug.

## Maintenance
- About 2 hours a week. Prefer clipped evergreens and gravel over large lawns.
