# Brief: Playground between apartment blocks, Sofia

Client interview (Phase 1). Original notes were in Russian.

## Site

- 8 x 8 m, between apartment blocks
- Flat
- North at the top of the drawing; a road runs along the south side
- Sofia, Bulgaria (continental climate)

## Concept

- Three elliptical islands joined by bridges
- Island 1: sandbox
- Island 2: slides
- Island 3: climbing
- Mixed ages, 0 to 12 years

## Surfaces

- Islands: rubber surfacing (EPDM)
- Between the islands: loose gravel
- Bridges: rubber planks

## Must-haves

- A fence with noise protection against the road (south)
- Ground lights
- Benches for parents around the edge
- Litter bins

## Constraints

- No height restrictions
- No budget limit; the drawing should look good
- Real project, intended to be built
