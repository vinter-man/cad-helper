# Brief: Japanese backyard

The first project made with cad-helper, used to develop the Japanese-garden elements.

## Site
- Plot 25 x 40 m, rectangular, flat. North is up.
- House 10 x 12 m, set towards the north-east corner, entrance on the south facade.
- One pedestrian gate on the south side; no vehicle access through the garden.

## Style
- Traditional Japanese garden: asymmetry, odd-numbered groups, stone and water as the
  main elements, intentional empty space (ma).
- Must have: koi pond, dry garden (karesansui), tea garden (roji) with a water basin,
  stepping-stone paths, stone lanterns instead of modern lamps, cloud-pruned pines,
  maples, cherries, bamboo, azaleas, moss.
- A covered timber veranda (engawa) along the house.

## Use and upkeep
- Adults only; quiet sitting, viewing, tea.
- Evening use: low, sparse light only.
- High upkeep is acceptable (clipped pines, raked gravel).
