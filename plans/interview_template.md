# Interview Template — Landscape Project

Ask ALL these questions before creating a design plan. Save answers to `projects/<name>/brief.md`.

If the user doesn't know an answer, note "not specified" and use reasonable defaults.

---

## 1. Site

- Plot dimensions? (length x width)
- Plot shape? (rectangle, L-shape, triangle, irregular?)
- Orientation — where is north? Which side faces the street?
- Terrain — flat or slope? If slope, which direction?
- Soil type? (clay, sand, rocky, fertile, unknown)
- Where is the entrance / driveway?

## 2. Climate & Location

- City / region?
- Climate zone? (Mediterranean, continental, subtropical, etc.)
- Average winter minimum temperature?
- Rainfall — dry or wet area?
- Wind — is it windy? Dominant wind direction?
- Sun — which parts of the yard get full sun / partial shade / full shade?

## 3. Existing Elements

- House — position, dimensions, how many floors?
- Garage / parking — where?
- Existing trees to keep?
- Existing structures (shed, pool, well)?
- Underground utilities — water, sewer, gas, electric lines?
- Views — good views to frame? Bad views to screen?
- Neighbors — how close? Need privacy screening?

## 4. Style & Aesthetics

- Preferred style?
  - Japanese (zen, niwaki, stone, water)
  - Mediterranean (terracotta, olive trees, lavender)
  - English cottage (lush borders, roses, winding paths)
  - Modern minimalist (clean lines, geometric, few plants)
  - Wild / naturalistic (meadow, native plants)
  - Mixed / no preference
- Color palette — warm tones? Cool? Neutral?
- Materials — natural stone? Concrete? Wood? Mixed?
- Formal or informal feeling?

## 5. Must-Have Elements

- [ ] Terrace / patio (size?)
- [ ] BBQ / outdoor kitchen
- [ ] Dining area (how many people?)
- [ ] Lounge / seating area
- [ ] Fire pit
- [ ] Water feature (pond, fountain, stream?)
- [ ] Pergola / gazebo
- [ ] Vegetable / herb garden
- [ ] Fruit trees (which?)
- [ ] Flower beds
- [ ] Lawn area
- [ ] Children's play area
- [ ] Storage / shed
- [ ] Parking
- [ ] Fence / wall (style?)
- [ ] Other: ___

## 6. Must-NOT-Have

- Any elements you definitely don't want?
- Allergies to specific plants?
- Fear of water (no pond)?

## 7. Usage

- Who uses the garden? (adults, children ages, elderly, pets?)
- How often do you host guests? How many people?
- Morning or evening garden use?
- Do you want to eat outside? How often?
- Work from garden (need quiet zone)?

## 8. Maintenance

- How much time per week for garden care?
  - Minimal (1 hr/week) → evergreens, gravel, low-maintenance
  - Moderate (3-5 hr/week) → lawn, some flowers, pruning
  - Intensive (daily) → roses, vegetable garden, topiary
- Will you hire a gardener?
- Irrigation — manual watering or automated system?

## 9. Budget & Priorities

- Budget range? (affects material and plant choices)
- What's most important to you? (rank 1-3)
  - Beauty / aesthetics
  - Privacy
  - Entertainment space
  - Low maintenance
  - Children's play
  - Food growing
  - Relaxation / meditation

## 10. Inspiration

- Any photos or examples you like?
- Gardens you've visited and admired?
- Specific plants you love?
- Colors that make you happy?

---

## After the interview

Save the answers to `projects/<name>/brief.md` and proceed to Phase 2 (Plan).
